I am an M.S. student at the Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI at KAIST, advised by [Prof. Beomjoon Kim](https://beomjoonkim.github.io/) in the [HuGe Lab](https://www.hugelab.org/) (Humanoid Generalization Lab). I work on robot learning, using reinforcement learning and sim-to-real transfer for manipulation and humanoid whole-body locomotion.

Before that, I received my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering and Computer Science from KAIST, graduating summa cum laude.

I expect to graduate in February 2027 and am looking for full-time positions. See my [projects](/projects/) for robot videos and my [CV](/cv/) for details.
